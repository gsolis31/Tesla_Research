## AI Chip Production (Samsung/TSMC)
### Latest Update (2026-03-13)
**⚠️ CRITICAL: AI6 Chip Delayed ~6 Months**: Tesla's AI6 chip production on Samsung's 2nm process has been pushed back roughly 6 months, delaying mass production to Q4 2027. A six-month slip on the MPW (multi-project wafer) run suggests Samsung still has yield or process maturity challenges to work through before 2nm is production-ready. [Source: [Electrek](https://electrek.co/2026/03/12/tesla-ai6-chip-delayed-samsung-2nm-production-slips/)]

**EUV Equipment Testing in Progress**: Trial operations of extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography equipment at Samsung's Taylor Fab 1 started in March 2026, setting the stage for mass production of Tesla's AI5 and AI6 chips. [Source: [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/samsungs-tesla-ai5-ai6-chip-factory-to-start-key-equipment-tests-in-march-report/)]

**Production Expansion Discussions**: Tesla is still discussing expansion of AI chip supply with Samsung, with purchasing executives scheduled to meet Samsung officials to negotiate additional production volume. Tesla requested an additional 24,000 wafers per month (from existing 16,000), which would bring total capacity to 40,000 wafers/month if finalized. [Source: [Teslarati](https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-expansion-samsung-ai6-production-plans-report/)]

## FSD Country Approvals
### Active Countries
**United States**: FSD commercially available (baseline)

**China**: FSD available with local regulatory compliance

**🎉 Netherlands/European Union (APPROVED March 4, 2026)**:
- Dutch vehicle authority (RDW) officially granted Tesla Extended Type Approval for FSD (Supervised) on March 4, 2026
- Approved under Article 39 of EU regulations, which allows individual member states to grant exemptions for advanced systems
- **This is the first EU country to officially approve FSD**
- EU-wide rollout now possible through mutual recognition mechanism without restarting approval process in each country
- Early Access program invitations already being sent to European owners
[Source: [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/tesla-fsd-supervised-tackles-dutch-city-traffic-eu-approval-nears), [EVXL](https://evxl.co/2026/03/01/tesla-fsd-supervised-approval-netherlands/), [Basenor](https://www.basenor.com/blogs/news/tesla-fsd-early-access-invites-hit-europe-what-to-do-now)]
